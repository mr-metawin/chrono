EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Chrono is an immutable onchain savings protocol engineered natively for the BOT
blockchain ecosystem. Built with pure Solidity (^0.8.24) and OpenZeppelin 5.x
primitives, Chrono solves the "hyper-liquidity temptation" dilemma of Web3 by
allowing users to lock native BOT until a specified future date.

Positions are strictly isolated, featuring custom labels, flexible or strict
exit modes, and mathematical finality. There are zero admin backdoors, no owner
roles, no pause toggles, and no upgradeable proxies. Once committed in Strict mode,
neither the user, nor any attacker, nor the contract deployer can access funds
prior to the unlock timestamp.

SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION - CHRONO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chrono introduces trustless, programmatic time-locking directly on the BOT blockchain:

- Strict Mode (Default):
  - Pure mathematical self-commitment. Once locked, funds cannot be withdrawn under
    any circumstance before block.timestamp >= unlockTime.
  - Zero admin overrides, zero emergency loopholes.

- Flexible Mode:
  - Emergency safety valve. Users can exit early if truly necessary by paying a
    pre-configured 10% penalty (1000 bps) credited to protocol penalties.
  - Transparent, automated, and strictly onchain.

- 100% Free at Unlock:
  - After unlockTime, 100% of the locked BOT is withdrawable by the owner with 0% fee.

- Pure Onchain Simplicity:
  - No backend, no IPFS pinning, no centralized database, and no oracle reliance.
    All lock data is stored directly in contract storage as plain UTF-8 strings.

SLIDE 6: TOKENOMICS & ECONOMIC MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Native Currency Exclusivity:
   - Operates natively in BOT. Zero synthetic wrapped tokens, zero secondary tokens,
     zero inflationary minting.

2. The 10% Early-Exit Safety Valve:
   - Users opting for Flexible mode accept a 10% penalty on early exit.
   - Penalty calculation enforces mathematical rounding-up:
     penalty = (amount * penaltyBps + 9999) / 10000; payout = amount - penalty.
   - Payout + Penalty always strictly equals the original locked principal.

3. Solvency & Conservation Invariant:
   - Contract balance strictly equals:
     totalLocked + penaltyBalance
   - Invariant verified through 128,000 automated calls across 256 fuzz runs with zero reverts.
